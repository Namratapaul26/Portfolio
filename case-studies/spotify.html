1. Executive Summary & Business Context

The Problem: Spotify listeners experience recurring frustration when recommendations "play it safe" and cycle familiar tracks ("Recency Trap" & "Old Song Resurrection"). Through market research and listening log analysis, we confirmed that ~70% of users notice repetitive recommendations, causing skip fatigue and eventual churn.

The Solution: An AI-Driven Emotion-Based Discovery Framework allowing users to specify mood/context, talk to an AI DJ, or use Smart Shuffle to discover novel gems. Success is measured by lifted novel track saves, longer session lengths, and reduced churn.

🌐 Market Leadership

Spotify leads global music streaming with ~31.7% market share, followed by Apple Music (12.6%), Amazon Music (11.1%), and YouTube Music (9.7%). Streaming is projected to reach $42.8B by 2026.

📱 Short-Form Competitors

Social platforms like TikTok (75% of Gen Z discover new music there) are becoming primary discovery engines. Spotify must bridge this gap through visual preview feeds & instant mood chips.

💡 Churn Economics

Spotify's annualized churn sits at ~30.9%. Retaining just 1% more Premium subscribers (~2M users at $4.55/mo ARPU) preserves ~$10M+ in annual recurring revenue.

2. Opportunity Sizing & Competitor Benchmark

Opportunity Sizing: Spotify's 239M Premium subscribers log ~20.7 hours of listening per week. Our AI DJ pilot demonstrated a +22% increase in session length. Enhancing discovery across 615M total users unlocks billions of additional streams, driving ad revenue and subscriber retention.

6. Technical & AI Emotion System Architecture

The technical architecture integrates a Mood Vector Embedding Engine with Spotify's existing BigQuery data pipeline and recommendation microservices:

INPUT PROCESSING

NLP & Emotion Classifier

Captures text or voice input ("feeling dreamy"), extracting key mood tags & intensity vectors via LLM fine-tuned model.

MOOD ENGINE

Hybrid Collaborative Filtering

Injects mood vectors into collaborative filtering model, biasing recommendations toward matching mood/style tags without losing user taste.

DATA PIPELINE

Real-Time Event Logging

Logs all play, skip, save, and mood feedback events into BigQuery. Offline analysis continuously refines mood mapping accuracy.

SCALABILITY

High QPS & Caching Layer

Pre-generates & caches popular mood playlists on AWS/GCP to serve millions of daily discovery requests with <2s latency.
