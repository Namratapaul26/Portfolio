1. Executive Summary & Problem Breakdown

The Problem: Quick commerce relies heavily on maintaining a strict <10-minute delivery promise. Stockouts on high-velocity items (milk, bread, fresh produce) and checkout delays lead to immediate order cancellation and app abandonment.

Strategic Solution: Introduced a Predictive Dark Store Inventory Rebalancing Algorithm combined with 1-tap checkout prompts and dynamic surge-free routing. Reduced average picker packing time from 140s to 75s per order.
