1. Executive Summary & Business Context

The Activation Bottleneck: Only ~35% of new Groww users currently finish the mandatory digital KYC process (PAN, Aadhaar, bank verification, selfie e-sign) and make a first investment. This indicates a major onboarding bottleneck where ~65% of signed-up users churn before becoming active investors, undercutting growth and wasting marketing acquisition spend.

📊 Market Growth

India's retail investor base is booming—Demat accounts grew from ~4 crore in 2020 to ~14 crore by 2024. Nearly 48% of retail market participants are under age 30, preferring fast, mobile-first digital experiences.

🟢 Groww's Market Position

Groww is India's largest retail brokerage with 26.6% market share and ~14.8M active users by mid-2026. Having achieved ~3x revenue growth (FY23–FY25), closing onboarding gaps is critical to sustaining top-line expansion.

⚔️ Competitive Landscape

Top rivals like Zerodha achieve 50–55% signup-to-first-trade conversion with 35-minute video KYC, whereas Groww averages ~35% conversion and ~45-minute KYC processing times.

2. Problem Statement & Opportunity Sizing

Problem Statement: Groww’s onboarding funnel suffers significant drop-off: ~65% of sign-ups abandon the app during mandatory KYC or pre-trade hesitation. Financial terminology overload, lack of guidance, and PAN/document verification friction lead to high acquisition waste.

Opportunity Sizing: Improving conversion from 35% to 50% on 3 million annual new sign-ups yields ~450,000 additional active investors. Given Groww's monetization model (broking and mutual fund fees), this translates directly into a multi-crore top-line revenue surge.

3. User Research Insights & Personas

Qualitative interviews with novice and hesitant investors revealed 4 primary friction points: Education & Confidence Gap ("How do I pick a stock?"), Complex Interface (overwhelming terms like F&O), KYC Frustration (PAN memory hurdles, selfie failures), and Financial Anxiety.

6. Product Requirements Document (PRD) & User Stories

📱 Registration & KYC Module

Phone/OTP login, visual "Step 2 of 5" progress indicator, inline tooltips ("What is PAN?"), auto-fill via DigiLocker APIs, fallback manual upload path.

🤖 AI Assistant ("Groww Guru")

Overlay chat bubble active across onboarding screens. Responds to FAQs ("forgot PAN", "how to link bank") and escalates to support seamlessly.

💡 Post-KYC Trading Landing

Displays "Congratulations, you're verified!" followed by 1-click starter mutual fund packs and a direct "Fund Your Account" button.

User Stories

  • ✓ As a new user, I want a progress bar during KYC so that I know how many steps remain.
  • ✓ As a beginner investor, I want contextual tips like "What is PAN?" so I can complete forms without confusion.
  • ✓ As a hesitant user, I want to ask an in-app AI assistant questions ("How do I link bank?") and get instant help so I don't drop off.
  • ✓ As a prospective trader, I want curated starter portfolios so I feel confident executing my first investment.

7. Technical & AI System Architecture

The system leverages a modern microservice stack with a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) AI pipeline:

FRONTEND

React Native Mobile App

iOS and Android native cross-platform build with smooth animations & sub-2s screen loads.

BACKEND

Node.js / Express Microservices

KYC processing, document uploads, DigiLocker & vendor OCR liveness SDK integration.

AI / RAG PIPELINE

Python + GPT-4 + RAG

OpenAI LLM fine-tuned on Groww help docs with vector search (ChromaDB) for sub-second answers.

INFRASTRUCTURE

AWS EC2 + S3 + PostgreSQL

Auto-scaling EC2, S3 document storage, PostgreSQL relational data, InfluxDB event logging.
