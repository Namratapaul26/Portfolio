5. Decision Journal ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Documented trade-off reasoning behind major product and technical architectural decisions:

DECISION FRAMEWORK

RAG Architecture vs Fine-Tuning Decision

Context: Deciding how to ground AI Meeting Assistant on domain transcripts with low latency and zero hallucinations.

Options Evaluated: (A) Fine-tuning LLaMA 3, (B) RAG pipeline with ChromaDB vector search + Mistral AI.

Trade-offs: Fine-tuning incurs high retraining costs for changing data; RAG provides instant document updates and exact citation provenance.

Final Decision: Implemented RAG pipeline with Whisper + ChromaDB + Mistral AI.

Expected Impact & Reflection: Sub-second response time and 80% reduction in reporting effort achieved.
